# Fundamental Research

Use web/browser capabilities when available.

## Source hierarchy

Prefer:
1. Central banks and official government/statistics sources.
2. Exchanges, regulators, and official economic-calendar sources.
3. Company/issuer primary releases where relevant.
4. Reputable financial news.
5. Secondary commentary only as context.

For every material fact, record source and time. Distinguish:
- observed data;
- source interpretation;
- Hermes inference;
- uncertainty.

Never fabricate current data when web access is unavailable.

For macro analysis, consider relevant factors such as rates, inflation, labor data, growth, liquidity, currencies, yields, commodities, and scheduled events depending on the symbol.
